# FINSHIELD
FINSHIELD is an AI-powered financial investigation platform that helps users make more informed financial decisions throughout the consumer journey.

It combines four modules:
● Loan Explorer: Compares loan offers based on total borrowing cost, disclosed fees,
repayment terms and eligibility.
● Agreement Decoder: Converts complex loan agreements and KFS documents into
plain-language explanations and interactive cost breakdowns.
● Offer Verifier: Investigates lender identity, claimed associations, website domains and
financial offers using evidence from authoritative sources.
● Complaint Guide: Organizes evidence and guides users through relevant official
complaint procedures.

The platform integrates TinyFish to retrieve live web evidence and an AI layer to interpret
documents, explain financial terms and prepare personalized investigation reports.

Technology: React, Python/FastAPI, PostgreSQL, an LLM API, document
parsing and TinyFish for live web investigation.

Innovation: Instead of offering generic financial summaries or a simple scam/safe
verdict, FINSHIELD investigates specific offers and documents, compares claims
against official evidence, explains financial consequences and clearly identifies
unresolved questions.

The impact of FINSHIELD is not just helping users find lower interest rates. It is helping them
understand what they are agreeing to, identify information that needs verification, and take
better-informed next steps.
